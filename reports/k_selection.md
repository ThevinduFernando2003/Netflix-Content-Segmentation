# Choosing k

Generated by `scripts/select_k.py`. Re-run it to refresh every number.

## Decision: k = 6 on `r1`

Chosen k=6 as the finest k in 5..10 whose mean bootstrap ARI is at least 0.80. Bootstrap ARI is 0.8203 ± 0.1211. Stability outranks a flattering silhouette: a compact cluster that moves under resampling is noise. k below 5 is not allowed to set the ceiling, because an ARI near 1.0 there is the stability of a coarse partition rather than a content segmentation. Criteria that disagreed: maximum bootstrap ARI at k=2 (0.9996), a coarser cut excluded from the ceiling; highest ARI inside 5..10 at k=5 (0.9075); inertia knee at k=9; max silhouette at k=2 (0.1733); max Calinski-Harabasz at k=2; best (lowest) Davies-Bouldin at k=19.

The elbow is a heuristic, not evidence. It is plotted and located with `KneeLocator`, which returned k=9. Gaussian mixture BIC (diagonal covariance) is lowest at k=20 (BIC=-7694559.2). Those model-selection curves are allowed to disagree with the stability choice; the report says so instead of averaging them into a false consensus.

k=2, k=3 and k=4 are scored, then excluded from the ceiling. The table below is the adjusted Rand index between a spherical k-means fit on the product representation (which is allowed to see `type`) and the `type` column. A value near 1 means that k rediscovered movies versus series.
