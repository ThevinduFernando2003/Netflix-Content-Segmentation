# Representation design

Generated by `scripts/design_representation.py`. Every score below comes from that run.

## Product representation

**r1 — Genre + numeric + categorical (no free text).**

r2 has the highest bootstrap ARI (0.732) but leads R1 (0.725) by under 0.05. The catalogue has no synopsis, so the text block is titles only. R1 is the product: it is simpler, interpretable, and statistically tied on the metric that decides whether clusters exist.

The deciding metric is bootstrap adjusted Rand index, not silhouette. A compact cluster with unstable labels is noise. Silhouette on this catalogue is expected to sit roughly between 0.05 and 0.25; that is normal for overlapping genres and is not treated as a failure.

## What the file actually contains

There is no `description` and no `cast`. The text block is the title, L2-normalised TF-IDF (1–2 grams, `min_df=5`), reduced with TruncatedSVD (not PCA) and L2-normalised again so Euclidean k-means is spherical k-means. See `reports/data_profile.md`.

## Duration

The numeric block uses `duration_normalised`: the training-set percentile of minutes among movies, or of seasons among series. Median-imputing both `duration_minutes` and `duration_seasons` would hand every movie the median season count and every series the median runtime, and k-means would split on that artefact. Clustering the two types separately was the alternative; it was rejected for the product model because the catalogue is one segmentation problem and `type` is an observed attribute, not a leak.

## Block dominance and one-hot geometry

Blocks are scaled to unit total variance, multiplied by `sqrt(weight)`, concatenated, then L2-normalised per row. Default weights are `{'text': 1.0, 'genre': 1.0, 'categorical': 0.5, 'numeric': 0.5}`. A raw `hstack` of 20,000 TF-IDF columns with eight numeric columns is not used.

One-hot categories sit at a Euclidean distance of sqrt(2) from each other, so India is exactly as far from Japan as from the United States. That geometry is arbitrary. The categorical block is therefore down-weighted, and Gower distance plus KPrototypes are reported as mixed-type alternatives. They are comparisons, not the serving model: Gower was computed on a subsample because the full float64 matrix would be about 0.62 GB.

## SVD explained variance

TruncatedSVD kept **150** components and explained **0.459** of TF-IDF variance. 150 is the configured width; the component count is capped by the rank of the title matrix when the vocabulary is smaller. The curve is `reports/figures/explained_variance_svd.png`. Components are L2-normalised after the SVD, which is the usual LSA step so Euclidean distance tracks cosine.
