- **Spherical KMeans.** Primary. L2-normalised rows, Euclidean k-means.
- **MiniBatchKMeans.** Same k. At this row count the speed gain is real and the quality loss is the point of the comparison.
- **Agglomerative Ward.** Euclidean Ward on the dense representation. Ward is not used with cosine.
- **Agglomerative average cosine.** Average linkage with cosine distance on a fixed 4,000-row subsample (condensed float64 distances). Ward is not used with cosine. Scores for this row are on that subsample. The product labels are still the full-data spherical k-means.
- **GaussianMixture diag.** Soft assignments. Diagonal covariance because a full covariance is ill-conditioned at this width.
- **DBSCAN.** eps=0.8083 from KneeLocator on the k-distance curve, min_samples=15. HDBSCAN was not used: it pulls in a compiled build and is a poor fit for the runtime image. DBSCAN is the density-based result, including the noise fraction.
- **BisectingKMeans.** Optional hierarchical-divisive check on the same spherical representation.

## Density-based result

DBSCAN found **1** cluster and marked **0.0015** (0.1% of titles) as noise, with eps=0.8083. The k-distance knee is high enough that almost every title falls in one component. There is no density gap that recovers the six segments. On this spherical representation, density-based clustering collapses. That is a negative result, not a parameter that was tuned until it looked like k-means.

The k-distance curve is `reports/figures/dbscan_k_distance.png`.

Dendrograms: `reports/figures/dendrogram_ward.png` and `dendrogram_cosine.png`. They show nested structure. The product model is still a flat spherical k-means at the chosen k.
