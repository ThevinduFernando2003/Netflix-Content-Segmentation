# Cluster profiles

Generated by `scripts/evaluate.py`. Genres are ranked by lift (cluster rate / catalogue rate), not by raw frequency. Terms are class-based TF-IDF on the titles in each cluster (the extract has no synopsis). Representative titles are the five rows closest to the spherical k-means centroid. Boundary titles are the five lowest silhouettes in the cluster.
